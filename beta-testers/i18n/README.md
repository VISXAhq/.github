# VISXA beta tester invitation — languages

These public information translations explain how to volunteer for the invitation-only UK Standard Visitor visa preparation beta, the VISXA service-fee waiver for selected/confirmed testers, and the distinction between VISXA fees and UK government or other third-party costs.

The only currently offered beta interfaces are English and Arabic. These GitHub translations do not mean the product or website has launched in the languages listed below. They are editorial drafts and should be reviewed by competent native speakers before broader marketing or product use.

## Important safeguards

- Initial private beta is by invitation; early testing uses sample documents only.
- Do not submit real passports or personal case documents for the test.
- The VISXA preparation-service fee is waived only for selected and confirmed participants. UK government visa fees and third-party costs are not waived.
- No visa, appointment, or invitation is guaranteed.
- For current information and private waitlist registration, use [English VISXA](https://visxa.com/en) or [Arabic VISXA](https://visxa.com/ar).
- For public non-personal ideas only, use [the VISXA beta issue](https://github.com/VISXAhq/.github/issues/1).

# VISXA UK Standard Visitor visa — Beta tester invitation

VISXA, Inc. is inviting volunteers to help improve its English- and Arabic-language UK Standard Visitor visa application-preparation experience. This early-access programme is invitation-only, beginning with particular interest in people in Egypt and welcoming interest from other countries.

If selected for an agreed beta participation, your VISXA application-preparation service fee will be waived. Official UK government visa fees, application centre charges and other third-party costs are not waived.

## What is VISXA?

VISXA helps people organize answers and supporting information for a UK Standard Visitor visa application in English or Arabic. Its goal is to make preparation clearer, more accessible and easier to review.

The applicant stays in control of their answers and, when a real-application workflow is available and approved, makes the actual submission to the UK government. VISXA does not decide visas and does not provide immigration legal advice.

VISXA is a separate Delaware, USA company, independent of Global Expat, Inc. and of the UK government, UKVI, VFS Global and visa application centres.

## What will beta volunteers test?

- Clarity of instructions, navigation and questions
- How easy it is to use in English and Arabic
- Understanding of document checklists and common steps
- Explanation of fees, privacy, and what VISXA can and cannot do
- Bugs, confusing wording, accessibility, and suggested improvements

The current testing stage uses sample documents only. Do not upload genuine passports, national IDs, financial evidence or real visa-case details. Joining the waitlist does not give immediate access or authorize a real visa submission.

## Fee waiver: what is and is not free?

| Fee or activity | Beta offer |
| --- | --- |
| VISXA preparation service fee | Waived for people selected and confirmed for the beta programme |
| UK government visa application fee | Not waived; if you later apply, you pay the government directly |
| Visa application centre services, translation, travel or other third-party expenses | Not waived |
| Visa approval, appointment, processing time | Never guaranteed |

VISXA is not currently taking beta payments, and not every waitlist participant will receive an invitation. Any later real-application service would require its own confirmed readiness, terms and consent.

## Who should express interest?

People thinking about short trips to the UK for tourism, seeing family, business meetings or other Standard Visitor purposes; individuals who can help test English or Arabic usability; and people who want to help a new product become more useful. If you are unsure whether you need a visa or a UK ETA, the official [GOV.UK website](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa) is the place to verify.

## How to volunteer

1. Visit [VISXA English](https://visxa.com/en) or [VISXA Arabic](https://visxa.com/ar).
2. Join the official early-access waitlist with an email address you control. You can request your language and select visitor visas.
3. Read the site's current Terms and Privacy Policy before joining.
4. The VISXA team may contact selected people when places are available, explaining the scope and conditions of testing.
5. For public, non-personal questions and general feedback, use the [GitHub beta-testing discussion](https://github.com/VISXAhq/.github/issues/1).

Do not post your email, phone number, passport number, visa history, documents, financial information, or other personal details on GitHub. The GitHub thread is public.

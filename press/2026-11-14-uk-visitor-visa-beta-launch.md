FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — 14 November 2026

VISXA opens its UK visitor visa preparation beta, in English and Arabic.

People can apply to join at visxa.com. VISXA approves a limited number of applicants each day and helps them prepare their own UK Standard Visitor visa application; applicants submit it themselves on GOV.UK.

WILMINGTON, Delaware — VISXA, Inc., an independent technology company, has opened a public beta of its service for preparing UK Standard Visitor visa applications, in English and Arabic.

People apply to join at visxa.com. A person at VISXA reviews every application, and VISXA approves up to 10 new applicants a day during the beta. Approved applicants sign in to prepare their application: a personal document checklist built from their own answers, secure uploads of their real documents (each file is virus-checked), and drafts such as a cover letter and itinerary that they review and approve.

The applicant stays in control. They submit the visa application themselves on GOV.UK and book their own visa application centre appointment. VISXA does not submit applications, book appointments or make visa decisions.

VISXA's fee is £49 per applicant for the first 100 paying applicants (founding price), then £79. The UK government's visa fee is separate and is paid directly on GOV.UK. Personal information is handled under VISXA's published privacy notice.

Mohamed Aboshanab, Founder of VISXA, said:

> We want to build this around the people who will actually use it. As we grow, we want to listen, understand where the preparation process becomes confusing and develop an experience that is clearer and more accessible in both Arabic and English.

What "beta" means is explained at https://visxa.com/en/beta-info: a real, working service with limited places, fixed prices and no guarantee of any visa outcome.

VISXA, Inc. is an independent US company. It is not affiliated with UK Visas and Immigration, the UK Home Office, VFS Global, TLScontact or any government authority. VISXA does not provide immigration advice and does not guarantee visa approval; UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) alone decides visa applications.

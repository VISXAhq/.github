# VISXA — visa application preparation and global mobility education

VISXA, Inc. | English | 2026-11-26

## What is VISXA?

VISXA is an independent US technology company helping with UK Standard Visitor visa preparation during a limited-access beta. It is not a government authority, does not provide immigration advice and cannot guarantee a visa decision. Check the official VISXA website for current availability.

## How can I prepare responsibly?

Start with the relevant government's official guidance. Organise your trip purpose, dates, planned costs and relevant supporting evidence. Check the current requirements; a generic checklist may not cover your circumstances. Keep identity documents and application details private.

## What are common terms in visitor application preparation?

Supporting evidence — مستندات داعمة: documents that substantiate stated information. Itinerary — خطة الرحلة: planned activities and dates. Sponsor — جهة أو شخص داعم: someone contributing support, where relevant. Eligibility — الأهلية: criteria that apply to a route. Decision — القرار: determination by the competent authority. These are plain-language explanations, not official legal translations; meanings vary by jurisdiction.
